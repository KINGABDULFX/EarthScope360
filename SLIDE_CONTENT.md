## Slide 2 — The problem
Environmental datasets are large and difficult to interpret.
People need a simple way to ask: what is changing, where, how quickly, and is the trend statistically meaningful?

## Slide 3 — Our solution
EarthScope 360 lets a user choose a Nigerian location, an Earth variable and a time period, then turns NASA data into a clear trend investigation.

## Slide 4 — How it works
1. Select location and variable.
2. Retrieve monthly NASA POWER data.
3. Aggregate monthly values into annual averages.
4. Visualize the time series and fitted linear trend.
5. Estimate trend significance with a Mann–Kendall test.

## Slide 5 — NASA data and science
NASA POWER Monthly Point API.
T2M, PRECTOTCORR and RH2M.
MERRA-2-derived meteorological estimates.
OLS slope + basic Mann–Kendall test.
Data caveat: gridded estimates, not direct station observations.
